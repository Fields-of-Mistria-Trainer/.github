> [!IMPORTANT]
> Fields of Mistria supports co-op farming. Options marked **`session`** affect the shared farm, and **host-only mode ships enabled** to block them while you are a guest.
>
> Changing the season on someone else's farm affects everyone's crops — agree before you toggle.

## What it does

Fields of Mistria is a cozy farming sim — plant crops, raise animals, befriend villagers, and restore a magical valley. The fun is the slow life. The chore is the stamina bar and waiting for crops.

This trainer removes the wait. **Unlimited stamina** lets you work all day, **instant crop growth** skips seasons, and **max friendships** unlocks all heart events. **Unlock all animals and upgrades** gives you the perfect farm from year one.

## Hotkeys

`Insert` opens the menu · `End` resets everything · `F1`–`F11` as above, all rebindable

> [!TIP]
> **Instant crop growth** + **season control** = harvest any crop in any season. Grow pumpkins in spring and strawberries in autumn.

> [!WARNING]
> **Season changes** reset crop states. Save before toggling if you have growing plants.
